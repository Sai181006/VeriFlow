## Recommendation for the supervisor

Choose the **paper type first**. If the target is a full research paper within
about a month, ICDE research round 2 is the closest topical match, but the
reviewer concerns in [the council note](council-review-2026-10-08.md) should be
settled before committing to it. PVLDB's 1 November cycle is earlier and has a
monthly later-cycle option if a stronger evidence package needs more time.
If the desired output is a shorter system paper, assess the ICDE demo route
against an actual interactive walkthrough. Do not treat DEFT as a shortcut for
the current technical paper.

The practical decision requires the supervisor's venue preference, acceptable
paper type, and whether submission just beyond 8 November counts as “within a
month.” Once selected, apply the exact template, page limit, disclosure and
artifact rules from that venue's current call. No submission has been made.
